How This App Works
💰 Balance Breakdown

Your Total Cash is split to protect your business: Mpamba (reserved to restock), Profit (safe to spend/save), Capital (your base money), and Owed (money belonging to 3rd party suppliers).

📦 Stock & 3rd Party

Phones you bought. If you select "Third Party", it adds stock without reducing cash. When sold, the cost automatically moves to the Owed bucket.

🔄 Trade-In

Simultaneously sell your phone and add the customer's phone to your stock, calculating the net cash difference perfectly.

Spent Money

Record transport, airtime, or select "Owed to Supplier" to safely clear your 3rd party debts.

🔒 Savings

A safe place to keep profit aside so you don't spend it by mistake.
